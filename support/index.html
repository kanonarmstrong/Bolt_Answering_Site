Frequently asked questions

Will my customers know they are not talking to a person?

Yes. Bolt always tells your caller it’s a smart assistant, right up front. It never pretends to be something it’s not. Some pros worry that upfront disclosure will scare callers off. In practice, most callers care more about getting a fast, helpful answer than about who’s on the other end. Bolt is built to be useful in the first ten seconds, not to trick anyone.

Can it handle a real emergency, or just routine calls?

Bolt’s smart assistant is trained to recognize the calls that can’t wait. No heat or no A/C, carbon monoxide fumes, flooding or water damage, a burst pipe, a sparking outlet, and similar serious situations. When a call like that comes in, Bolt gets the details and escalates it to you right away: a push notification, then a text, then an automated call if you still haven’t seen it. What Bolt doesn’t do is make the judgment calls that need your expertise on site. It’s built to flag and get you moving fast, not to diagnose or promise a timeline your customer can hold you to.

Do I have to give up my phone number?

No. You keep your number, whatever number you already give out to customers. Most pros run their business off their cell phone, so Bolt is built around that: you forward your existing number to your smart assistant, the same way you’d forward calls to voicemail. No separate business line required. You can turn forwarding off anytime by dialing a code.

How much does it cost, and is there a contract?

Your first month is free. The next three months are discounted. No long-term contract. See current pricing.

How long does setup take? Do I need a computer?

About 10 minutes, start to finish, right from your phone. No laptop required. You can do it when you’re at the pump.

What happens to my calls if I cancel my Bolt account?

Depends which one you do.

Cancel and your smart assistant keeps working through the end of your current billing cycle. You’ve already paid for it, so it keeps answering until then. After that, it stops.

Delete your account and your service still runs to the end of that same paid-through cycle. Deleting doesn’t cut you off early or bill you again. The difference is what happens after: cancel just stops the service, and delete erases your account and all its data for good.

Either way, one thing doesn’t happen automatically: your calls keep forwarding to Bolt’s number until you turn forwarding off with your carrier. It’s the same code you used to turn it on, so dial it again to send calls straight back to your phone.

Will it actually get the details right, or will I end up with garbage leads?

Bolt’s smart assistant captures what you’d want to know before calling someone back: name, callback number, address, what’s going on, and how urgent it is. And every single call comes with a full transcript, so if anything’s unclear or incomplete, you can see exactly what was said and fill in the gap yourself. Nothing’s hidden behind a summary you have to trust blindly.

How is this different from a human answering service?

A human answering service works, but it can cost over $1,000 per month and the receptionists can be off the clock sometimes too. Bolt is 24/7/365, provides you summaries and detailed transcripts of every call, and costs a fraction.

What’s the difference between this and other ‘AI receptionists’?

Other answering services either don’t know your business or they can cost thousands of dollars per year and require you to buy a bunch of software you don’t use (or want). Bolt gives our pros the tool they need to not miss a job and integrates with the tools they already use (phone, sms, email) to make sure they get Day One value.
