Frequently asked questions

What is Bolt Answering?

Bolt Answering is an AI phone answering service for solo tradespeople. It answers every call, books jobs and follows up.

Who is it for?

Solo independent pros and small crews in HVAC, plumbing, electrical, general contracting, and handyman work. That includes people building a business on the side of a full-time job, and owners whose spouse or a friend used to cover the phone.

Will my customers know they are not talking to a person?

Yes. Bolt always tells your caller it’s a smart assistant, right up front. It never pretends to be something it’s not. Some pros worry that upfront disclosure will scare callers off. In practice, most callers care more about getting a fast, helpful answer than about who’s on the other end. Bolt is built to be useful in the first ten seconds, not to trick anyone.

Will my customers hang up when an assistant answers instead of me?

Some will. A few callers hang up on anything that isn’t a person, and no setting changes that. But the real comparison isn’t Bolt versus you. It’s Bolt versus your voicemail while you’re under a sink.

Bolt answers in your business’s name, introduces itself as your smart assistant, and if a caller asks, tells them it’s an AI. It speaks English or Spanish, depending on how the caller starts talking. Every call shows up in the Calls tab with a summary and transcript, including the ones that hung up, so you can call those people back yourself.

Can it handle a real emergency, or just routine calls?

Bolt’s smart assistant is trained to recognize the calls that can’t wait. No heat or no A/C, carbon monoxide fumes, flooding or water damage, a burst pipe, a sparking outlet, and similar serious situations. When a call like that comes in, Bolt gets the details and escalates it to you right away: a push notification, then a text, then an automated call if you still haven’t seen it. What Bolt doesn’t do is make the judgment calls that need your expertise on site. It’s built to flag and get you moving fast, not to diagnose or promise a timeline your customer can hold you to.

Do I have to give up my phone number?

No. You keep your number, whatever number you already give out to customers. Most pros run their business off their cell phone, so Bolt is built around that: you forward your existing number to your smart assistant, the same way you’d forward calls to voicemail. No separate business line required. You can turn forwarding off anytime by dialing a code.

How much does it cost, and are there contracts, hidden fees, or a catch?

Your first 30 days are free. To start, you verify a card. Bolt places a temporary $1 hold that’s returned automatically, and your card isn’t charged during the trial. On day 30 your monthly subscription starts automatically. It’s month to month, with no annual contract. See current pricing.

How long does setup take? Do I need a computer?

About 10 minutes, start to finish, right from your phone. No laptop required. You can do it when you’re at the pump.

What happens to my calls if I delete my account?

Delete your account in Settings → Subscription and your smart assistant keeps answering until the end of the period you’ve paid for. After that, the service stops, and your account and all its data are erased for good.

One thing doesn’t happen automatically: Your calls keep forwarding to Bolt’s number until you turn forwarding off with your carrier, so turn it off when your service ends. Go to Settings → Call Forwarding and tap the deactivate forwarding button to turn off forwarding with your phone company.

Will it actually get the details right, or will I end up with garbage leads?

Bolt’s smart assistant captures what you’d want to know before calling someone back: name, callback number, address, what’s going on, and how urgent it is. And every single call comes with a full transcript, so if anything’s unclear or incomplete, you can see exactly what was said and fill in the gap yourself. Nothing’s hidden behind a summary you have to trust blindly.

What if it gets the details wrong or books two jobs at the same time?

Before Bolt offers a time, it checks when you’re busy on your main Google Calendar, plus your work hours, your time off, and the gap you set between jobs. If two callers try to book the same time, the first one gets it and the second is offered a different time. Right before it books, Bolt checks your calendar once more for that exact time.

You can read the summary and full transcript of every call, and change or cancel any job from your schedule. If Bolt can’t reach your calendar, it doesn’t guess. It takes the caller’s details so you can call them back.

How is this different from a human answering service?

A human answering service works, but it can cost over $1,000 per month and the receptionists can be off the clock sometimes too. Bolt is 24/7/365, provides you summaries and detailed transcripts of every call, and costs a fraction.

What’s the difference between this and other ‘AI receptionists’?

Other answering services either don’t know your business or they can cost thousands of dollars per year and require you to buy a bunch of software you don’t use (or want). Bolt gives our pros the tool they need to not miss a job and integrates with the tools they already use (phone, sms, email) to make sure they get Day One value.

Is Bolt Answering the same as other companies called ‘Bolt’?

No. Bolt Answering is an independent product at boltanswering.com and isn’t affiliated with other companies that use the name Bolt.
