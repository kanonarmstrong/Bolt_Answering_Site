Bolt How-To’s

Temporarily disable spam blockers on iPhones

  1. 1. Open Settings
  2. 2. Scroll down and tap Apps
  3. 3. Scroll down and tap Phone
  4. 4. Scroll down to Screen Unknown Callers, and select Never
  5. 5. Scroll down to Call Blocking & Identification, turn off Silence Junk Callers.
  6. 6. Turn off third-party call blocking apps (Verizon Call Filter, Hiya, Truecaller, etc.) if you have them installed.
  7. 7. Turn off Call Filtering filtering: Scroll down to the Call Filtering section, then turn off Unknown Callers.
  8. 8. After you have completed your test call...
    1. Add the phone number that called you to your Contacts
    2. Turn your blockers back ON. (... because robocalls suck.)
