Disable Live Voicemail on iPhones

The Live Voicemail feature may be intercepting your test call before the call is forwarded to your assistant. Follow these instructions to disable Live Voicemail.

  1. 1. Open Settings
  2. 2. Scroll down, and tap Apps
  3. 3. Scroll down, and tap Phone
  4. 4. Scroll down, and tap Live Voicemail and the switch to OFF
  5. 5. (Verizon Users) Reset Call Forwarding (Verizon users only)
    1. Dial *73
    2. Wait for several beeps, then hang up
    3. Return to the Bolt App
    4. Tap Settings
    5. Tap Call Forwarding
    6. Setup Call Forwarding again
