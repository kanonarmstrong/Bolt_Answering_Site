Turn off spam blockers on Android

Some Android and carrier settings filter calls from numbers you haven’t saved — including your Bolt demo call. Turn these off for a minute, then head back to Bolt and tap Call me again.

  1. Turn off “Filter spam calls”

    Open the Phone app, tap ⋮ (More) → Settings → Caller ID & spam, and turn off Filter spam calls. (Exact wording varies by phone — on Samsung it’s Phone → Settings → Block numbers / Caller ID and spam protection.)

  2. Check Do Not Disturb

    Pull down the quick settings and make sure Do Not Disturb is off — it can silence calls from people who aren’t in your contacts.

  3. Pause your carrier’s call filter

    If you use your carrier’s spam app — AT&T ActiveArmor, Verizon Call Filter, T-Mobile Scam Shield, or similar — open it and turn off automatic blocking while you try the demo.

Done? Head back to Bolt and tap “Call me again.” You can turn these settings back on right after your demo call.
