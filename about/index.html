About Bolt AnsweringAbout us

When the heat goes out or a pipe bursts, a family calls you. Bolt makes sure that call reaches you.

Bolt answers on the phone and number you already have. It books the job, screens out spam, and sends you the details so you can keep working.

The phone always rings at the wrong time

A handyman is stuck in a meeting at his day job. An HVAC tech is in a crawl space. A contractor is walking a job with a homeowner. A plumber’s phone is in the van.

Every one of those calls is a job. Most callers won’t leave a voicemail. They call the next name on the list.

A receptionist costs more than $2,000 a month. So the phone falls to a wife, girlfriend, or friend, until their own jobs and kids need them back. We built Bolt for that stretch.

What Bolt does on every call

  1. Answers day or night, and tells callers it’s your assistant.
  2. Screens out spam and robocalls.
  3. Asks what the job is, where, and how soon. Flags urgent calls.
  4. Books the job while the caller is on the line.
  5. Sends you the details by text or app notification. No voicemails.
  6. Speaks Spanish and 70+ other languages.

Who we serve

Solo pros and small crews in HVAC, plumbing, electrical, general contracting, and handyman work, including people running a business on the side.

How Bolt is different

  1. Your phone, your number. Forward calls from your cell. Customers dial the same number. Setup takes under 10 minutes.
  2. Runs from your pocket. Everything lives on your phone. No desk needed.
  3. Knows the trades. It can tell an emergency from a routine job.
  4. Priced for a growing business. Less than $4 a day after your free trial.
  5. Honest with callers. Most callers would rather book than leave a message. Customers are speaking to AI receptionists every day, so our assistants keep it real and are honest with your callers.
